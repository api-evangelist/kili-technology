Standard benchmarks provide snapshot assessments in artificial conditions rather than continuous monitoring in live environments. They focus on technical metrics that data scientists understand rather than business outcomes that stakeholders care about.The evaluation frameworks used to validate AI systems answer "Does this model work?" when organizations need to know "Will this model deliver value in our specific context?" This gap between benchmark performance and production success reflects a fundamental misalignment between how we evaluate AI systems and what we need them to do.
